Residential & Commercial Cleaning in Ottawa, Ontario

Prestige Cleaning Co helps homeowners, renters, property managers, offices, clinics and local businesses maintain cleaner, more comfortable spaces. We provide dependable cleaning throughout Ottawa, with flexible service for apartments, houses and commercial properties.

What areas around Ottawa do you serve?

Our primary service area includes Ottawa, Nepean, Kanata, Barrhaven, Orléans and Stittsville. Contact us to confirm service for your neighbourhood.

Do you clean offices and commercial properties?

Yes. We provide commercial cleaning for offices, clinics, retail spaces, property-managed buildings and other Ottawa businesses.

Can I request a one-time cleaning?

Yes. One-time cleaning is available for seasonal refreshes, special occasions or whenever your home or business needs extra attention.
